\section{Scope, Ethics, And Validation Path}

Aggregate-only reproducibility is a scoped audit mode for sensitive evidence. It supports reviewer inspection of claim-evidence alignment, artifact completeness, metric definitions, agreement summaries, consistency checks, and figure regeneration from aggregate inputs. Row-level qualitative re-analysis remains a governed local validation layer when public release would expose sensitive speech or transcript content. The CDS-ASR case study demonstrates the framework in one high-stakes speech-to-decision setting; broader validation can extend it across additional domains, reviewer teams, and release policies.

The framework works alongside institutional review, data-use agreements, retention policy, encryption, access control, and deployment governance. Its contribution is a claim-evidence governance structure for reviewer-auditable aggregate evidence under sensitive-data constraints.
