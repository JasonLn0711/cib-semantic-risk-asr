\section{Limitations And Ethics}

Aggregate-only reproducibility is weaker than row-level reproducibility. Reviewers cannot independently inspect raw transcript cases. The framework depends on honest local execution, institutional controls, and accurate manifests. The CDS-ASR case study is one project, not universal validation of the framework.

This paper does not claim legal compliance and does not replace IRB review, data-use agreements, retention policies, encryption, or access control. It provides a claim-evidence governance framework for reviewer-auditable aggregate evidence under sensitive-data constraints.
