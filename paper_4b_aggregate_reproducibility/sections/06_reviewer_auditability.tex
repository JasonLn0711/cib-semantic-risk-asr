\section{Reviewer-Auditable Boundary}

Reviewer-visible auditability is not the same as full row-level reproducibility. Reviewers can inspect aggregate artifacts, figures, tables, manifests, validation gates, claim registries, and release-boundary statements. They cannot independently inspect raw transcript cases in the public package.
