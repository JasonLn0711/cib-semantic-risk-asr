\section{Discussion}

Aggregate-only reproducibility is useful for sensitive speech AI domains where open row-level release is inappropriate: medical speech, fraud calls, psychotherapy speech, law-enforcement-adjacent speech data, and high-stakes conversational AI evaluation. The framework gives reviewers a structured way to inspect the public evidence layer without requiring public release of transcript-bearing materials.

The framework also clarifies what aggregate auditability can and cannot support. It can support claim-evidence alignment, artifact completeness checks, metric-definition checks, consistency checks, and figure regeneration from aggregate inputs. It cannot support independent qualitative review of hidden local-only transcript rows.
