\section{Discussion}

Aggregate-only reproducibility is useful for sensitive speech AI domains where public row-level release requires strong governance: medical speech, fraud calls, psychotherapy speech, law-enforcement-adjacent speech data, and high-stakes conversational AI evaluation. The framework gives reviewers a structured way to inspect the public evidence layer while transcript-bearing materials remain under declared controls.

The framework clarifies the positive audit surface. It supports claim-evidence alignment, artifact completeness checks, metric-definition checks, agreement inspection, consistency checks, and figure regeneration from aggregate inputs. Qualitative transcript review becomes a local governed validation layer rather than a missing public artifact.
