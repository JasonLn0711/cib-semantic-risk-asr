\section{Discussion}

\subsection{From Transcript Accuracy To Action Stability}

The results support a speech-evaluation contribution for high-stakes ASR: transcript accuracy and semantic similarity provide essential evidence layers, and decision stability adds the action-facing layer required by speech-to-decision workflows. Clinical-documentation studies show how ASR output can enter operational records and downstream care processes \citep{hodgson2016risks,blackley2019speech}; semantic ASR metrics show how evaluation can move from literal transcript overlap toward meaning and usability \citep{roy2021semantic,kim2022semdist}. CDS-ASR extends this progression by making action movement under plausible transcript alternatives measurable.

This shift is the main scientific contribution. WER and CER anchor transcript-model comparison. SRES represents semantic-risk evidence. CEIS measures whether a plausible ASR alternative around a decision-critical spoken atom moves the declared downstream action. The result is a layered evaluation view: transcript fidelity, semantic movement, and decision stability can be read together rather than collapsed into a single transcript score.

\subsection{What CEIS Adds To Existing ASR Evaluation}

CEIS contributes a counterfactual view of ASR risk. The score localizes attention to risk atoms, evaluates plausible alternatives around those atoms, and translates transcript movement into policy-space movement. This creates an interpretable bridge between recognition uncertainty and conservative action. Selective prediction and reject-option methods provide mature foundations for action under uncertainty \citep{chow1970reject,angelopoulos2021conformal}; CDS-ASR instantiates that idea for high-stakes ASR by identifying the spoken atoms where uncertainty reaches the action boundary.

The pilot predictor layer and aggregate replay show how this bridge functions operationally. CEIS reaches the strongest point AUC in the scoped pilot comparison and full recall at its diagnostic operating point. In aggregate replay, CEIS-triggered conservative action moves high-risk missed and critical miss counts to zero at the same diagnostic budget as the strongest semantic-risk trigger. These results position CEIS as a decision-stability companion to transcript and semantic metrics.

\subsection{Interpreting The Dual-Reviewer Ablation}

The dual-reviewer ablation clarifies the mechanism carried by CEIS. Full CEIS reaches AUC 0.717039 for strict decision-change labels for both reviewers, and the atom-neutral, plausibility-neutral, binary-atom, and top-3 variants remain tightly aligned. This pattern identifies the core signal as decision-changing risk-atom instability under plausible ASR alternatives. Plausibility and atom weights remain useful calibration and localization handles for future acoustic and domain-prior validation; the selected-300 evidence shows that the action-stability signal is already visible across simplified CEIS variants.

The reviewer agreement results strengthen this interpretation. The completed audit provides strong agreement for decision-change, semantic-risk, expected-safe-action, and confidence labels. This agreement turns the selected-300 surface into a reviewer-trustworthy evidence layer and gives the CEIS ablation a stable human-label foundation.

\subsection{Implications For High-Stakes Speech Systems}

The practical implication is direct: as ASR becomes part of routing, escalation, documentation, compliance support, and abstention workflows, evaluation should include the stability of the downstream action. Better ASR models improve transcript quality, and they also sharpen the remaining decision-stability question by concentrating attention on the spoken details that matter most. CDS-ASR provides a way to audit those details through risk atoms, plausible alternatives, CEIS scoring, and conservative policy replay.

For CSL readers, the contribution is a speech-evaluation layer rather than an anti-fraud-only application. The anti-fraud setting supplies a high-stakes Mandarin testbed where amount, actor, action, time, negation, uncertainty, intent, and scam pattern can carry the action boundary. The method generalizes as an evaluation design: identify spoken decision atoms, construct plausible ASR alternatives, map alternatives through a declared action function, and measure action stability with an auditable score.
