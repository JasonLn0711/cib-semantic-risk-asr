\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Transcript-centered ASR evaluation.}
WER and CER remain core ASR evaluation metrics because they provide auditable transcript-similarity measures. For Chinese-language ASR, this manuscript treats character-level reporting as the primary paper-facing accuracy layer and segmented word metrics as supplemental evidence. Clinical-documentation reviews also show why transcript quality matters in operational settings where spoken input becomes institutional record content \citep{hodgson2016risks,blackley2019speech}. The aggregate metric-compliance audit is recorded in \artifact{70_experiments/runs/wer_metric_audit_2026_05_25/journal_compliance_summary.json}.

\paragraph{Semantic and downstream-aware ASR evaluation.}
Semantic ASR metrics extend transcript-centered evaluation by asking whether a transcript preserves meaning or downstream task behavior. Semantic-WER proposes an end-usability transcript metric, and SemDist connects ASR quality to user perception and downstream NLU behavior \citep{roy2021semantic,kim2022semdist}. CDS-ASR builds on this direction and targets a specific decision-stability question: whether plausible ASR alternatives around decision-critical atoms would change downstream action.

\paragraph{ASR correction and confidence-aware repair.}
ASR correction and confidence filtering improve transcript usability and reduce avoidable repair risk. CDS-ASR complements correction by evaluating whether the remaining uncertainty interval around risk atoms is still decision-stable.

\paragraph{Selective prediction, reject option, and abstention.}
Selective prediction and reject-option methods provide background for conservative action under uncertainty \citep{chow1970reject,angelopoulos2021conformal}. In CDS-ASR, conservative action means abstention, review prioritization, or escalation for human confirmation. It is not an automated punitive or adverse-action decision.
