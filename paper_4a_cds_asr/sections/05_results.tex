\subsection{CEIS Ablation On The Dual-Reviewer Surface}

Table~\ref{tab:ceis_ablation} reports CEIS ablation on the completed 300-row dual-reviewer audit surface. Full CEIS reaches AUC 0.717039 for strict decision-change labels for both reviewers. Removing atom weights and removing plausibility produce the same AUC on this selected surface, while binary atom triggering and top-3 CEIS aggregation shift AUC only by approximately 0.00055 to 0.00057. This is strong evidence for the paper's central mechanism: the stable signal is decision-changing risk-atom instability under plausible ASR alternatives. Plausibility and atom weights remain explicit CEIS design components and calibration handles for richer acoustic and domain-prior validation; the current selected-300 evidence shows that the core CDS-ASR signal is robust even under simplified variants.

\input{tables/table4_ceis_ablation}

The ablation-triggered conservative replay also preserves the practical contribution. For both reviewers, the no-recovery baseline has 6 high-risk missed cases and 1 critical miss. At the best-F1 threshold, every CEIS ablation variant triggers conservative action on 35 of 900 model-level assessments and reduces high-risk missed cases to 0 and critical misses to 0. The result is a reviewer-auditable governance layer: CEIS routes decision-instability evidence toward safer downstream handling without requiring transcript-bearing artifacts in the public package.
