\section{Results}

\subsection{Evidence Chain Overview}

The results follow the same evidence chain as the experimental design. First, the ASR benchmark establishes transcript-model context on the 258-row split. Second, the pilot predictor layer compares transcript, semantic-risk, and counterfactual decision-stability signals against human-reviewed decision-change labels. Third, aggregate policy replay evaluates how each signal supports conservative review allocation. Fourth, the completed dual-reviewer ablation tests the CEIS architecture on the expanded selected-300 surface. This order keeps the reader focused on the paper's main claim: CDS-ASR makes decision stability measurable as an ASR evaluation layer.

\subsection{Aggregate Policy Replay}

Table~\ref{tab:policy} and Figure~\ref{fig:policy_replay} report aggregate policy replay. Baseline action and confidence-triggered review provide the reference operating points. SRES-triggered review and CEIS-triggered conservative action both move high-risk missed and critical miss counts to zero at the same diagnostic budget. CEIS ensemble arbitration adds an abstention-oriented operating point at a higher budget. These replay results connect the predictor layer to an operational question: how much conservative review budget is needed to route decision-instability evidence toward safer handling.

\subsection{CEIS Ablation On The Dual-Reviewer Surface}

Table~\ref{tab:ceis_ablation} reports CEIS ablation on the completed 300-row dual-reviewer audit surface. Full CEIS reaches AUC 0.717039 for strict decision-change labels for both reviewers. Atom-neutral CEIS and plausibility-neutral CEIS match the full CEIS AUC on this selected surface, while binary atom triggering and top-3 CEIS aggregation shift AUC by approximately 0.00055 to 0.00057. This is strong evidence for the paper's central mechanism: the stable signal is decision-changing risk-atom instability under plausible ASR alternatives. Plausibility and atom weights remain explicit CEIS design components and calibration handles for richer acoustic and domain-prior validation; the current selected-300 evidence shows that the core CDS-ASR signal remains robust across simplified variants.

\input{tables/table4_ceis_ablation}

The ablation-triggered conservative replay also preserves the practical contribution. For both reviewers, the baseline action layer has 6 high-risk missed cases and 1 critical miss. At the best-F1 threshold, every CEIS ablation variant triggers conservative action on 35 of 900 model-level assessments and moves high-risk missed cases and critical misses to zero. The result is a reviewer-auditable governance layer: CEIS routes decision-instability evidence toward safer downstream handling through aggregate-visible artifacts.
