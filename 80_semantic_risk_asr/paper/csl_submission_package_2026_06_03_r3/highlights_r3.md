# Highlights

- Introduces CEIS as a counterfactual decision-stability layer for high-stakes
  Mandarin ASR.
- Evaluates plausible transcript alternatives around decision-bearing risk
  atoms under a declared downstream triage action space.
- Reports final selected-300 / 900-assessment aggregate regeneration with
  pre-declared failover to adjudicated decision-change AUC / complementarity.
- Positions CEIS as complementary to SRES rather than as a replacement or
  total-superiority claim.
- Provides aggregate-only reproducibility artifacts while keeping sensitive raw
  audio, transcripts, row identifiers, hypotheses, and reviewer sheets local.
